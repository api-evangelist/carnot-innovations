# Carnot Innovations

> Carnot Innovations is a Hong Kong-based building energy management company that applies AI, machine learning, and advanced data analytics to optimize HVAC systems and reduce energy consumption across commercial buildings, data centers, hospitals, railways, and sewage treatment facilities in the Asia-Pacific region. The company is a portfolio company of 500 Global. It does not currently publish a public developer program or API; this profile exists in the API Evangelist network for discovery and enrichment.
